# Design Read

Reading this as: a trust-first consumer comparison product for price-sensitive AI buyers, with an editorial utility language, leaning toward custom Tailwind CSS, Geist typography and restrained motion.

## Dials

- DESIGN_VARIANCE: 6
- MOTION_INTENSITY: 3
- VISUAL_DENSITY: 6

## System

- Background: paper white and graphite
- Accent: one signal lime
- Typography: Geist Sans + Geist Mono
- Radius rule: cards 18px, controls 10px, pills fully rounded
- Motion: short entrance and tactile button feedback only
- Layout: asymmetric hero, dense offer tables, no generic dashboard card grid
- Trust: source links, timestamps, stock status and disclaimers remain visible
